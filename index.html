About Techealth

Making India's Roads Safer with AI

Techealth Apex Private Limited is a deep-tech startup headquartered in New Delhi, India — on a mission to eliminate preventable road accidents and transform how India's commercial vehicle industry operates.

We combine computer vision, edge AI, and real-time telemetry to give fleet operators and logistics companies unprecedented visibility into vehicle safety and driver wellbeing.

Founded by engineers passionate about road safety in India, our team brings together expertise in AI, automotive systems, and logistics to build products purpose-fit for Indian conditions.
